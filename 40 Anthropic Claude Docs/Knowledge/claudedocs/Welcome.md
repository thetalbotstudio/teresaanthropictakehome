## Which product do you need?

Start from what you’re trying to do:

[“I want Claude to see my files, Slack messages, calendar, or codebase”Connectorsaccess to your tools and data](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/overview) [“I want Claude to read my Outlook mail and OneDrive files”Microsoft 365 connectoryour Microsoft 365 data in chat, not the Office apps](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/microsoft/365) [“I want to turn a document into a slide deck”Claude for M365drafting decks, docs, and email inside the Office apps](https://claude.com/docs/office-agents/overview) [“I want Claude to do this task the way my team does it”Skillsinstructions and scripts for a task](https://claude.com/docs/skills/overview) [“I want to share a ready-made setup with my whole team”Pluginsbundled skills and connectors for Cowork and Claude Code](https://claude.com/docs/plugins/overview) [“I want my whole channel to see and steer Claude’s work”Claude TagClaude in your team’s Slack](https://claude.com/docs/claude-tag/overview) [“I want Claude to run through my own cloud provider”Claude on third-party platformsClaude Desktop through your cloud provider](https://claude.com/docs/third-party/claude-desktop/overview) [“I want to build or publish a connector”MCPbuild on the open protocol and submit to the directory](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/building/mcp) [“I’m using Claude Code in my terminal”Claude CodeClaude in your terminal and IDE](https://code.claude.com/docs)

## Create an account

1Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai/)

2Select **Continue with Google** or enter your email address

3Follow the prompts to complete registration

All plans have access to the [Connectors Directory](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/directory). Free plans can also add one [custom connector](https://claude.com/docs/connectors/custom/remote-mcp). Compare [Claude plans and pricing](https://claude.com/pricing).

Claude for Government access is set up by your agency’s administrators, so you don’t need to create an account. See the [Claude for Government guide](https://claude.com/docs/government/overview).
